Instructions, a few

What is the Room

A voice made from Antonio Flamini's books Il Vuoto (The Void). It says little, asks questions and leaves space. The books are in Italian and almost entirely blank; this page is in English.

The Room
Choose the volume, write a thought and press Leave. Hush leaves an empty space, Room gives you a small exercise.
The counter
The silence rises in Volumes I and II and falls in III. Every answer can be copied or saved as an image.
The Test
Five questions, one of seven archetypes, your "page N of 150", images to share.
Today
One question a day, the same for everyone. Your note stays only on your device. You can pass the question on to someone, without your answer.
Erase
Write a sentence: the page erases it letter by letter and leaves the longest word.
Games
Small one-minute games about emptiness, with a result to share.
Pass
Ready-made lines to send to one person, with the link.
Every day
Install it on your phone: it's free, no app store or account needed, and it works offline too. The "Install the app" button is at the top and at the bottom of the page. In the Today room you can also get a reminder of the question every evening, from your phone's calendar.
The presence
An ink membrane that breathes, listens, goes quiet and speaks. With the voice on, it reads the answers aloud.
Two ways to answer
Live voice: inside Claude, what you write is sent to Claude's AI, with your account, to prepare the answer. Fragment voice: answers written in advance, everything stays on the page. It always works.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't save conversations: close the page and they're gone. Only your preferences, your last Test and your Today notes stay on your device.
It isn't Antonio and it isn't a therapist.
It doesn't give you the whole book: it quotes only short lines, the rest is on Amazon.
If you're thinking of hurting yourself: call your local emergency number (911 US, 999 UK, 112 EU), or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.
